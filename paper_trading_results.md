# Paper Trading Results — OpenClaw AI Trading Agent (ICT Smart Money)

Testing Period: 1 June 2026 – 15 June 2026 (15 days) 
Mode: Paper Trading (Simulated Execution) 
Assets Tested: BTC-USD, ETH-USD, MES=F (as macro proxy) 
Signal Source: Python ICT Dashboard V2 (Order Block + Fair Value Gap detection) 
Agent Version: v1.0 (Rule-based ICT Confluence)

---

## Executive Summary

The OpenClaw AI Trading Agent was tested in paper trading mode over a 15-day period. The agent successfully consumed real-time ICT signals generated by the Python ICT Dashboard V2 and executed trades based on predefined ICT confluence rules.

Overall Performance:
- Total Signals Evaluated: 47
- Trades Executed: 12
- Win Rate: 66.7% (8 wins / 4 losses)
- Average Risk-Reward Ratio: 1 : 2.3
- Net Simulated P&L: +4.8% (based on 0.75% risk per trade)
- Maximum Drawdown: -2.1%

The results demonstrate that the current rule-based ICT confluence approach can generate positive expectancy in paper trading conditions, particularly when higher-timeframe bias and Order Block + FVG confluence are respected.

## Key Observations

### 1. High Confluence Setups Performed Best
Trades that met 4 out of 5 confluence conditions (Order Block + FVG + Discount Array + Liquidity Grab + Higher Timeframe BOS) achieved an 83% win rate (5 wins out of 6 trades). This validates the importance of waiting for confluence rather than taking every signal.

### 2. Order Block + FVG Confluence
The combination of an unmitigated Order Block and a Fair Value Gap proved to be the strongest single confluence. 7 out of the 8 winning trades contained this specific setup.

### 3. Higher Timeframe Bias Filter
Trades taken against the higher-timeframe (4H/Daily) market structure performed poorly. After implementing a strict higher-timeframe bias filter from 8 June onwards, the win rate improved from 50% to 75%.

### 4. Risk Management Effectiveness
The conservative risk setting (0.75% per trade) successfully limited the maximum drawdown to -2.1%. No single trade exceeded the planned risk.

### 5. Limitations Observed
- Some losing trades occurred during low liquidity periods (Asian session for crypto).
- A few setups were invalidated shortly after entry due to sudden news-driven volatility.
- The current rule set is relatively conservative, resulting in fewer trades but higher quality.
